# OpenGame Game Jam 2026: Rules Reference

Jam ID for the CLI: `global-ai-game-jam-2026`

**Deadline: December 18, 2026.** The timezone hasn't been announced, so plan to submit by Dec 17.

## Dates

- The jam runs Nov 18 to Dec 18, 2026.
- Submissions open on opengame.io/jam on Nov 18.

## Eligibility

- Entrants must be 18 or older.
- Entries are individual only. No teams, and no splitting work across accounts.
- The jam is open worldwide, except where law or sanctions prohibit it.
- Winners must pass identity, eligibility and tax checks before payout.
- Each person can submit up to **2 games**.

## Game Requirements

- **Desktop web browser only**, played with mouse and keyboard. Controller support is optional.
  - These are out of scope: mobile, touch-only, native executables, Steam downloads, wallets, installers, VR and other special hardware.
- **Any engine is allowed as long as the final game runs in a desktop browser.** Three.js, Unity, Godot and custom engines all qualify, and Unity and Godot web exports are fine.
- The game must be uploaded to OpenGame and playable directly on the platform.
- It must run without installs, wallets or special hardware.
- **It must be a new game, started and finished during the jam.**
  - Game-specific gameplay, levels and finished systems built before the jam aren't allowed.
  - Reusable assets, libraries, tools and general frameworks are allowed.
  - Concept notes made before the jam are a gray area.
- **It must be a complete, playable experience, not a prototype or tech demo.** A short and simple game is fine if its loop is cohesive and polished.
- **AI coding agents are allowed**, including Claude, Codex, Grok, Cursor and others. You don't have to use OpenGame's own creation tools.
- **Fairness:** OpenGame may ask for development history, source code or other evidence that the game was made during the jam.
  - Keep a clean git history from the start.

## Genres (tracks)

Each game picks one primary genre, and OpenGame can reclassify an entry that doesn't fit its genre. The ten genres are:

1. Shooter & Survival
2. Action & Adventure
3. RPG
4. Platformer
5. Strategy
6. Simulation & Management
7. Racing & Sports
8. Fighting
9. Puzzle & Casual
10. Party, Social & Sandbox

## Prizes

| Prize | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Best Game Overall | $50k total: 1st $25k, 2nd $17.5k, 3rd $7.5k. Every entry is eligible, whatever its genre. |
| Each genre | $5k per genre: 1st $2.5k, 2nd $1.5k, 3rd $1k. |

- **Each game can win only one cash prize.**
- Sponsor credit prizes can stack on top of a cash prize.
- **The sponsors are ElevenLabs (voice and audio), fal (generative media) and MeshyAI (3D models).** Each sponsor gives "best use" credit prizes, and the amounts haven't been announced.

## Judging

- **A panel of 20+ judges from tech, AI and gaming will score the entries.** Their names haven't been announced.
- No rubric has been published. The stated bar is a complete, playable and thoughtfully crafted game.
